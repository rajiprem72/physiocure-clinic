Low Back Pain Treatment – Causes, Symptoms, Physiotherapy & Exercises

Learn how physiotherapy can effectively relieve lower back pain, improve mobility, strengthen your spine, and help you return to a pain-free lifestyle without surgery.

What is Low Back Pain?

Low back pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal problems affecting people of all ages. It may develop suddenly after lifting heavy objects or gradually due to poor posture, prolonged sitting, obesity, weak core muscles, or age-related degeneration. Most people experience low back pain at least once during their lifetime.

The good news is that the majority of low back pain cases respond very well to physiotherapy. Early diagnosis and proper rehabilitation can reduce pain, improve movement, and prevent future episodes.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent pain in the lower back
  • Muscle stiffness and tightness
  • Pain while bending or lifting
  • Difficulty standing or walking for long periods
  • Pain after prolonged sitting
  • Reduced flexibility
  • Muscle spasms
  • Pain radiating towards the hips or legs

Common Causes

  • Poor sitting posture
  • Long hours of desk work
  • Weak abdominal and back muscles
  • Lifting heavy objects incorrectly
  • Lumbar muscle strain
  • Disc degeneration
  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • Obesity
  • Sports injuries
  • Age-related spinal changes

Simple Home Exercises

After assessment by your physiotherapist, the following exercises are commonly recommended:

  • Pelvic Tilts
  • Knee to Chest Stretch
  • Cat-Camel Stretch
  • Bridging Exercise
  • Bird Dog Exercise
  • Lumbar Extension Exercises
  • Hamstring Stretch
  • Core Stability Exercises

Tips to Prevent Low Back Pain

  • Maintain proper posture while sitting.
  • Avoid sitting continuously for long hours.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Strengthen your core muscles.
  • Lift heavy objects correctly.
  • Maintain a healthy body weight.
  • Sleep on a supportive mattress.
  • Take frequent stretching breaks while working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can physiotherapy completely cure low back pain?

Yes. Most cases improve significantly with proper physiotherapy, exercises, and posture correction.

How many sessions are required?

Depending on the condition, recovery usually takes between 6 and 12 physiotherapy sessions.

Should I take complete bed rest?

No. Prolonged bed rest is usually not recommended. Controlled movement and physiotherapy help recovery.

When should I consult a physiotherapist?

If your back pain lasts more than a few days, limits your daily activities, or keeps returning, consult a physiotherapist as early as possible.
