TREATMENT TECHNIQUES

Physiotherapy Treatments & Techniques

At PhysioCure Clinic, different physiotherapy techniques may be used according to the patient's condition, symptoms, comfort and rehabilitation goals.

Traction Therapy

Traction therapy is a physiotherapy technique that may be used to help reduce discomfort, stiffness and pressure-related symptoms affecting the neck or back. Treatment is selected according to the individual's condition and assessment.

IFT – Interferential Therapy

Interferential Therapy (IFT) uses electrical stimulation as part of physiotherapy care. It may be used to help manage pain and support muscle relaxation when appropriate for the patient's condition.

Wax Therapy

Paraffin wax therapy provides gentle heat that may help improve comfort and reduce stiffness in selected conditions. It can be particularly useful as part of physiotherapy care for certain joint and soft-tissue problems.

Ultrasound Therapy

Therapeutic ultrasound uses sound waves as part of selected physiotherapy treatments. It may be incorporated into a treatment plan to help manage pain and support recovery of affected tissues.

Exercises & Manual Therapy

Therapeutic exercises and manual therapy can help improve movement, flexibility, strength and functional ability. Exercises are selected and progressed according to the patient's condition and rehabilitation needs.

Home Visit Physiotherapy

For patients who find it difficult to travel to a clinic, physiotherapy can be provided at home. Home visits can be especially helpful for older adults, patients with mobility difficulties and those recovering at home.
